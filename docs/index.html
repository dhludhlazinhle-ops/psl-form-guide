PSL Form Guide
How the numbers are made

Every team starts at an Elo rating of 1500. After each match the winner takes points from the loser, and the bigger the upset the bigger the swing. The home side gets a 60-point head start when predicting, and every August all ratings drift 25% back toward 1500 because squads change.

The fine print
  • Win, draw and loss probabilities split the Elo expectation with a simple heuristic that makes draws likelier between evenly matched teams. It is an estimate, not a fitted model.
  • The constants are standard starting values and have not been tuned for the PSL.
  • Goal margin is ignored, so a 4-0 and a 1-0 move ratings equally.
  • Probabilities are statistical estimates, not betting advice.
